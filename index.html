Cookie Policy

Warraq uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and your choices.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites remember your preferences and activity. They typically contain:

  • Session identifiers
  • User preferences
  • Authentication tokens
  • Analytics data

2. Types of Cookies We Use

2.1 Essential Cookies

  • Purpose: Authentication, security, platform functionality
  • Duration: Session or 12 months
  • Opt-out: Cannot be disabled; required for the platform to work

2.2 Performance Cookies

  • Purpose: Analytics, usage patterns, performance monitoring
  • Duration: 90 days to 2 years
  • Tools: Google Analytics, Mixpanel
  • Opt-out: You can opt out in cookie preferences

2.3 Functional Cookies

  • Purpose: Remember preferences, language, theme
  • Duration: 1 year
  • Opt-out: You can opt out in cookie preferences

2.4 Marketing Cookies

  • Purpose: Personalized ads, retargeting, social media integration
  • Duration: 6 months to 2 years
  • Partners: Google Ads, Facebook Pixel
  • Opt-out: You can opt out in cookie preferences or ad settings

3. Similar Technologies

We also use:

  • Local Storage: Browser storage for user preferences and session data
  • Web Beacons: Pixel tags in emails to track opens
  • Server Logs: IP addresses and access patterns

4. Your Cookie Preferences

4.1 Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit Warraq, a consent banner appears. You can:

  • Accept All: Allow all cookies
  • Customize: Choose which cookies to allow
  • Reject Non-Essential: Disable marketing and analytics only

4.2 Browser Settings

Most browsers allow you to:

  • View stored cookies
  • Delete cookies
  • Disable cookies entirely (not recommended—may break features)
  • Block third-party cookies

See your browser's settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) for instructions.

4.3 Do Not Track (DNT)

If your browser has DNT enabled, we respect this preference for third-party marketing cookies.

5. Google Analytics

Warraq uses Google Analytics to understand user behavior. Google may use your data for personalized advertising. You can opt out via:

  • Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on
  • Google Ad Settings

6. Third-Party Advertising

We work with advertising partners (Google, Facebook) who may use cookies to show you personalized ads based on your interests. You can:

  • Opt out in your Google Ad Settings
  • Opt out in your Facebook Ad Preferences
  • Use the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out tool

7. International Users

EU Users (GDPR): Cookies require explicit consent before non-essential usage.

California Users (CCPA): You have the right to know, delete, and opt-out of the sale of personal information.

8. Cookie Changes

We may add new cookies as we improve the platform. This policy will be updated accordingly, and you'll receive notice of material changes.
